# Lists Practice

Three days of hands-on list practice, one folder per day. Work through each folder's files in number order.

## Every day

- Write your prediction in the `# Prediction:` slot before you run anything.
- `03` has three rungs. Everyone does rung 1; rungs 2 and 3 are extra practice.
- `04` is for everyone. `05` and `06` are extra practice.
- At the end of `02`, write the rule from class in the `# Rule:` slot. Use it in `03` through `06`.
- ✅ Check in with an instructor after `02` and after `04`.
- Stuck for 5 minutes, or after two different tries? Raise your hand and have ready: the line, what you predicted, what happened, and what you tried.

## Day 1: Build a result (`day1/`)

Goal: build a new list from an old one, inside a function.

## Day 2: Stacks and safe lookups (`day2/`)

Goal: take items out of a list from the front, the back, or by name, without crashing.

## Day 3: Order and take the top few (`day3/`)

Goal: put a list in order, take the top or bottom few, and choose whether the original list changes.
